ADHD Print Task Manager

A specialized task management system designed for people with ADHD and similar neurodivergencies, featuring thermal printing for physical task reminders.

ADHD-Focused Features:

  • Thermal printing for physical task reminders
  • 3-level nested subtasks for breaking down overwhelming projects
  • Priority levels (Low, Normal, Urgent, Critical)
  • Due dates with overdue indicators
  • Periodic/recurring tasks for routine building
  • Designed specifically for ADHD executive function support
  • Physical task cards reduce digital overwhelm
  • Completion rituals for dopamine rewards
