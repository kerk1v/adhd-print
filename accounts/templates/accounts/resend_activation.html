Troubleshooting Tips
Check Your Spam Folder

Sometimes activation emails end up in spam or promotions folders. Check there first before requesting a new link.

Links Expire After 7 Days

If you registered more than a week ago, your link has expired. That's totally normal - just request a fresh one!

Use the Same Email

Make sure you're using the exact same email address you used when registering. Email addresses are case-sensitive.

Already Activated?

If your account is already activated, you'll get a message saying so. Just try logging in with your username and password instead.
